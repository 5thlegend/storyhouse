# 🎥 Storyhouse — Demo Recording Script (~2:45)

**[DO] = what to click.  [SAY] = read this aloud.**

## Pre-flight (30 sec before you hit record)
- Open **http://localhost:5173** in Chrome, make the window large.
- Send one throwaway message to wake the model, then **refresh** (clean screen, model warm).
- Start the recorder: **Loom** (webcam on — your face sells "for a friend") or **Win+Alt+R** (Game Bar).
- Family passcode for the family-view beat is: **`family`**

---

## ① HOOK — 0:00–0:20  *(your face, or over the glowing orb)*
> **SAY:** "My grandmother is in her eighties, with early Alzheimer's. She's carrying eighty years of stories almost none of us have written down. I didn't want to build another chatbot — I wanted to build somewhere her stories could *live*. This is Storyhouse."

---

## ② THE CORE LOOP — 0:20–1:05
**[DO]** Sit on the Living Room a beat.
> **SAY:** "It's not a chat window — it's a house you walk into. This is the Living Room."

**[DO]** By **voice** (or type): *"Do you remember what I told you about Grandpa's car?"* — let the reply stream in.
> **SAY:** "It answers only from what she's actually told it — grounded in her real memories, never made up. And it's all an open model, Gemma, running right here on the laptop. Offline. Private."

**[DO]** Type a new memory: *"My mother used to sing me an old Irish lullaby every night."* → click **Keep this story.**
> **SAY:** "She decides what's worth keeping."

---

## ③ WHAT MAKES IT MATTER — 1:05–2:05
**[DO]** Tap **📖 Library** → point to the new card + the green **"Her words"** badges.
> **SAY:** "Everything's preserved in her own words — and every fact is tagged: her words, versus an AI guess. It never blurs the two."

**[DO]** Open **"Moving to the house on Maple Street"** → scroll to the **two recollections**.
> **SAY:** "She said 1957, then later 1958. It keeps *both* — it never rewrites her memory to look tidy. That's the whole promise." *(slow down here)*

**[DO]** Tap **🖼️ Gallery** → let the orange-tree image land.
> **SAY:** "From a scene she described, an open-weight model painted this — and it's clearly labeled an interpretation, never a fake photograph."

**[DO]** Tap **🔒 Vault** → gesture at the stats + privacy lines → click **📖 Download storybook (PDF).**
> **SAY:** "And the family owns all of it — one click and it's a printable storybook of her life."

---

## ④ THE FAMILY — 2:05–2:25
**[DO]** Still in the Vault → click **Open Family View** → passcode **`family`**.
> **SAY:** "There's even a read-only view for the whole family — they can wander the Library, the Gallery, the Timeline…"

**[DO]** Point to the nav — no Living Room — and the purple banner.
> **SAY:** "…but her conversations and her microphone stay private to her. Enforced, not just hidden."

---

## ⑤ LAND IT — 2:25–2:45  *(back to your face if you can)*
> **SAY:** "It runs on a laptop, offline, on open models — her memories never leave the house. A closed API could've produced the conversation. It couldn't produce the *ownership*. That's the whole point of Storyhouse."

---

## Delivery notes
- **Slow down** on the Maple Street conflicting-date beat and the Gallery reveal — those are the "wow"s.
- Let the **streaming reply** play; don't talk over all of it.
- One take is fine — warmth beats polish.
- If a voice line misfires, just type it and keep rolling.
- When done: upload (YouTube/Loom unlisted), paste the link into the DEV post's **Demo** section where it says `[add link]`.
